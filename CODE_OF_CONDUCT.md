# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not
people.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised content,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption of discussions, or publishing
someone's private information.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer at **its.talep@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately. Depending on severity, the response ranges from a warning to a
permanent ban from the repository, and the maintainer's decision is final.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit comments.
