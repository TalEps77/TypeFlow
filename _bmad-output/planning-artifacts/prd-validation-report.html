Context: This validation was never run during planning. All 8 epics (25 FRs, Epics 1–7, plus ad-hoc Epic 8) are already implemented and marked done. This pass does not re-litigate scope — it looks for requirements that were never satisfied, never checked, or not actually testable as written, now that "done" is a claim rather than a plan.

The PRD itself is well-built as a requirements contract: FRs are numbered, nested, and mostly carry sharp Given/testable-consequence structure; Non-Goals, Assumptions, and Settled Decisions are explicit rather than implied; the Assumptions Index round-trips cleanly against inline tags; and the epics.md FR Coverage Map confirms all 25 FRs landed in a story.

Where it falls short is exactly where "done" needs the most scrutiny: the PRD's own definition of success (§7 Success Metrics) was never operationalized into any story, and appears never to have been measured — the flagship claim of the whole project (ivrit.ai beats the baseline on Hebrew WER, SM-1) has no verification step anywhere in epics.md or the retrospective. Two Open Questions the PRD explicitly gated on ("before Phase 1 closes," "before significant investment") also show no evidence of resolution despite Phase 1 having long since shipped. These are gaps in closing the loop, not gaps in the PRD's drafting quality.

Adequate

Trade-offs are named honestly throughout (e.g. FR-10's undo best-effort limitation, Command Mode's deliberate no-fallback rule in AD-4/FR-22). §9 Open Questions are genuinely open, not rhetorical. high

Two gate-conditioned Open Questions show no evidence of resolution despite the gate having passed

PRD.md §9.4, §9.5
§9.4 requires the ivrit.ai + Q4-LLM memory-headroom question be measured "before Phase 1 closes"; Story 1.3's verification only says it "can now be investigated" (instrumentation, not a result). §9.5 says AGPL distribution obligations are "worth confirming... before significant investment"; 8 epics have since shipped with no confirmation recorded anywhere.
Fix: Record the actual peak-memory measurement against OQ-4 and obtain/record the AGPL confirmation against OQ-5, or explicitly mark both "deferred, accepted risk" so the gate isn't silently missed.

high

Success Metrics (SM-1–SM-6) were never operationalized or measured

PRD.md §7; epics.md (absent)
SM-1 — "Word error rate... is lower with the ivrit.ai model than with large-v3" — is the claim the entire Phase 1 bet rests on. Searching epics.md and RETROSPECTIVE.md for SM-1 through SM-6 by ID finds zero hits; only the three counter-metrics (SM-C1–C3) are ever referenced, in unrelated manual-test notes. Story 1.1's manual step is "dictate a sentence, confirm text appears" — not a WER comparison. No held-out set is defined or referenced anywhere. The same absence holds for SM-2 (send-ready majority), SM-4 (latency budget), SM-5 (fallback bound), and SM-6 (corrections don't recur).
Fix: Add a lightweight story that actually runs the SM-1 WER comparison against a small defined held-out set, or explicitly relabel SM-1–SM-6 as "future validation criteria — not measured in this release" so the PRD doesn't imply they were checked.

Done-ness clarity

Thin

Most FR consequences are admirably precise (FR-16's normalization rules, FR-22's abort conditions). But several consequences — concentrated in timing/budget language — use unquantified adjectives the rubric explicitly flags, and the Success Metrics section has no defined measurement protocol at all, which also compounds the "untestable as written" problem beyond just soft language.

medium

SM-2/SM-3's evidence model conflicts with the PRD's own no-telemetry stance

PRD.md §7 SM-2/SM-3 vs §8, FR-8
SM-2 ("majority need no manual editing") and SM-3 ("still in daily use one month after ship") are outcome metrics that would normally need telemetry — but §8 bans telemetry/crash reporting/remote config outright, and History Records are explicitly local-only and excluded from diagnostic export (FR-8). The PRD never reconciles how these metrics could be measured without violating its own privacy stance.
Fix: State SM-2/SM-3 are self-assessed (fine for a one-user product) or specify a local-only, non-transmitted measurement mechanism.
medium

Several consequences use unquantified soft bounds

FR-6, FR-14, FR-25, SM-4
"a low single-digit-second timeout" (FR-6, never pinned even in epics.md Story 2.4), "negligible latency" (FR-14, FR-25, used twice with no bound), "a bounded character budget" (FR-14, no number), "a comfortable interactive budget" (SM-4, no number). These are the "reasonable performance" style phrasings the rubric calls out, even though most of the PRD is far more precise than this.
Fix: Pin at least a rough number per item (e.g. "≤3 seconds," "≤500 characters each side") so downstream stories have a bound to test against.

epics.md "Requirements Inventory" / "FR Coverage Map"
